01 / What it is

Not a conference.

Not a hackathon.

A program to ship something real.

Six weeksSix loopsOne island
I

Week 01

Break the ice.

Meet the people you'll build with. Share what you're working on, explore ideas, and start making things together.

Find your people.

II

Weeks 02—03

Go deep.

Small teams. Hard problems. Long stretches of uninterrupted work. Builders beside you who understand why the details matter.

Work all week.

III

Week 04 / Break week

Breathe.

Step away from the screen. Explore the island, rest, and let ideas settle. Come back with fresh eyes for the final stretch.

Room to breathe.

IV

Weeks 05—06

Ship it.

Turn the experiments into something people can use. Test it, fix the rough edges, and put it out into the world.

Make it real.

V

Friday = Demo Day

Show the work.

No decks. No theatre. Put working software in front of the room, learn fast, and return sharper on Monday.

Demo on Friday.

VI

Beyond the Cohort

Keep building.

Join a community of alumni who keep building together. Shipped apps, projects running in production, and collaborators to call on long after you leave the island.

The work continues.
